# YourMT3+ (vendored)

`amt/src` and `model_helper.py` are copied unchanged from the YourMT3 Hugging Face
Space (https://huggingface.co/spaces/mimbres/YourMT3, commit 5e66c1e, January 2025),
by Sungkyun Chang et al., licensed under the Apache License 2.0.
Paper: "YourMT3+: Multi-instrument Music Transcription with Enhanced Transformer
Architectures and Cross-dataset Stem Augmentation" (arXiv:2407.04822).

The `amt/src/tests` and `amt/src/extras` folders were left out. The checkpoint
("YPTF.MoE+Multi (noPS)", 562 MB) is downloaded at runtime into
data/models/yourmt3, not stored in this repository.
